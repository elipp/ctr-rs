About

ctr-rs is an online-focused port of Crash Team Racing (PS1, 1999), written in Rust.

It is heavily based on the ctr-native project, which is used to verify that the physics simulation and other mechanics remain as faithful to the original as possible. As we see it, the original CTR's feel and controls are truly S-tier, and replicating them is the absolute top priority for this project.

What's here

  • Exact physics Kart against ground and wall, and kart against kart, follow the original tick for tick. vehicle/tests/tick.rs checks them against the C reference, ctr-native. The weapons, crates and the level's objects follow the original closely.
  • Native performance, frame rates and aspect ratios The original's wobbly graphics have a charm of their own, but there is no need for PGXP or other workarounds, since there is an actual depth buffer.
  • Cross-platform It runs and builds on any system that Rust builds on, and in the browser.
  • No emulator hacks, ROM patches or launchers The current "CTR online" solutions have quite a bit of jank to get running.
  • Unlimited modding horizon Modding ctr-native is certainly possible, but difficult to get right, and limited in scope by its recomp-style codebase and the C language.
  • 30 Hz simulation, high-FPS rendering The game advances at the original's 30 ticks a second, independent of the frame rate it is drawn at.
  • Rollback netcode with no local delay Players connect directly (if possible) to each other with iroh and the QUIC protocol.
  • Custom tracks ctr-map turns an xdelta patch of the disc into a .ctrmap file that the client loads through the lobby.

Not planned

  • Adventure mode
  • Time trial
  • Relic race
  • Battle mode

How to play

You provide your own legally obtained disc image of Crash Team Racing (USA).

In the browser: press start and drop the .bin on the page. The image is read on your computer and is never uploaded. Needs a keyboard and a browser with WebGL2; the first load is a big download.

Natively: point CTR_BIN at the image, or put it at assets/ctr-u.bin, and run

cargo run -p client

Prior work

This project stands on the work of the CTR community, and could not exist without it.

  • ctr-native A native PC port of the game built on the decompilation. Our first reference for how the game behaves, and the oracle our physics tests compare against.
  • CTR-ModSDK The decompilation, symbols and headers that came out of it, which we use for the layouts of the game's data and for what ctr-native lacks.
  • CTR-Tools The community whose years of reverse engineering of the game's formats and code made both of those possible.
  • PsyCross And REDRIVER2, whose GTE (the PS1's geometry coprocessor) implementation is ported in crates/gte.

ctr-rs is a Rust port that works from these projects. It is not a fork of any of them.
